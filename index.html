Featured Provenance — Lot 002

"Steal not this horn for fear of shame, for on it is the owner's name."

Carved at Crown Point on 19 October 1761, this powder horn belonged to eight-year-old Dunham Pettit, gift of his brother John, a private in the Second Connecticut Regiment. Its carver is linked to only one other known horn — made for a Black soldier, Prince Negro, whose free status this association helps to confirm.
